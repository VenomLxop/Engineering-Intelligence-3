CONTRACT AGREEMENT
Project: Lakeview Water Treatment Plant, Phase II
Employer: Municipal Water Board
Contractor: ABC Infra Ltd.
Contract Value: INR 18,20,00,000
Date of Signing: 2025-11-01

Section 5.1 Scope of Work
The Contractor shall design, procure, and construct the Phase II clarifier units, filtration
gallery, and associated pumping infrastructure as per Schedule B drawings.

Section 6.1 Equipment Procurement
The Contractor shall procure and deliver the high-lift pumps to site by March 1, 2026. Delay
beyond this date shall attract a penalty of ₹30,000 per day, capped at 4% of Contract Value.

Section 6.4 Electrical Panel Supply
The Contractor shall supply and install the MCC panels by April 10, 2026, failing which a penalty
of ₹20,000 per day shall apply, capped at 3% of Contract Value.

Section 7.1 Civil Works Completion
The Contractor shall complete all civil works for the clarifier units by May 30, 2026. Delay
beyond this date attracts liquidated damages of ₹1,50,000 per week.

Section 7.3 Commissioning
The Contractor shall complete commissioning and handover of Phase II by August 15, 2026, subject
to a penalty of ₹40,000 per day for delay beyond this date, capped at 5% of Contract Value.

Section 9.2 Safety Compliance Reporting
The Contractor shall submit monthly safety compliance reports to the Employer by the 5th of each
following month.
