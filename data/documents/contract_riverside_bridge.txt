CONTRACT AGREEMENT
Project: Riverside Bridge Widening, NH-48 Segment
Employer: State Highways Development Authority
Contractor: XYZ Construction Pvt. Ltd.
Contract Value: INR 42,50,00,000
Date of Signing: 2026-01-15

Section 1. Definitions
"Contractor" means XYZ Construction Pvt. Ltd. "Employer" means the State Highways Development
Authority. "Engineer-in-Charge" means the officer appointed by the Employer to supervise the Works.

Section 4.1 Scope of Work
The Contractor shall design, procure, and construct the widening of the Riverside Bridge
superstructure and approach roads as per the drawings and technical specifications annexed as
Schedule A, including all structural steel, RCC works, and bearing replacement.

Section 6.3 Performance Guarantee
The Contractor shall furnish a Performance Guarantee of 5% of the Contract Value within 14 days
of the Letter of Award, in the form of a Bank Guarantee valid for the full contract period plus
the defect liability period.

Section 7.2 Material Delivery Obligations
The Contractor shall deliver all structural steel required for the superstructure to the project
site by October 15, 2026. Delivery shall be accompanied by mill test certificates and shall
conform to IS 2062 Grade E350. In the event of delay in delivery beyond the stipulated date, the
Contractor shall be liable to a penalty of ₹50,000 per day of delay, subject to a maximum of 5%
of the Contract Value, to be recovered from the Interim Payment Certificates.

Section 7.5 Cement and Aggregate Supply
The Contractor shall ensure continuous supply of OPC 53-grade cement and coarse aggregate meeting
IS 383 specifications throughout the concreting phase, with a buffer stock equivalent to no less
than 15 days of consumption maintained at site at all times.

Section 8.1 Quality Assurance
The Contractor shall conduct concrete cube testing at a frequency of not less than one set per
50 cubic metres poured, and shall submit test results to the Engineer-in-Charge within 3 working
days of each test.

Section 9.4 Time for Completion
The Works shall be completed within 18 months from the date of the Letter of Award, i.e. on or
before July 20, 2027. The Contractor shall submit a detailed CPM schedule within 21 days of
commencement and shall update it monthly.

Section 10.1 Liquidated Damages for Delay in Completion
Should the Contractor fail to complete the Works by the date specified in Section 9.4, the
Contractor shall pay liquidated damages of ₹8,00,000 per week of delay, subject to a maximum of
10% of the Contract Value.

Section 12.3 Safety Obligations
The Contractor shall deploy a full-time Safety Officer at site and shall conduct weekly toolbox
talks. Any fatal accident attributable to the Contractor's negligence shall entitle the Employer
to suspend the Works pending investigation, without extension of time being granted for the
suspension period.

Section 14.2 Change Orders
Any variation to the scope of Works exceeding 5% of the original Contract Value shall require a
written Change Order signed by both parties, specifying the revised cost and, where applicable,
a revised Time for Completion.

Section 16.1 Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising under this Contract shall first be referred to the Engineer-in-Charge for a
decision. If unresolved within 30 days, either party may refer the matter to arbitration under
the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
