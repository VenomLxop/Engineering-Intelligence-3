SITE INSPECTION REPORT
Project: Lakeview Water Treatment Plant, Phase II
Date of Inspection: 2026-09-10
Inspected By: Er. P. Nair, Engineer-in-Charge

Observations:

1. High-lift pumps were not delivered by the March 1, 2026 deadline under Section 6.1 and remain
undelivered as of this inspection, more than six months overdue. Contractor cites vendor
bankruptcy and is sourcing an alternate supplier.

2. MCC panels remain outstanding against the April 10, 2026 deadline under Section 6.4. Contractor
has not provided a revised delivery date.

3. Civil works for the clarifier units are approximately 70% complete against the May 30, 2026
target under Section 7.1, and are now overdue.

4. Commissioning under Section 7.3 cannot proceed until the above items are resolved and is
expected to miss the August 15, 2026 target significantly.

5. Monthly safety compliance reports under Section 9.2 have been submitted on time for the last
three months.

6. Overall assessment: this project shows a pattern of chronic delay across multiple procurement
and civil milestones, distinct from isolated single-item slippage.
