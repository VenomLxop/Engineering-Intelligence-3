SITE INSPECTION REPORT
Project: Riverside Bridge Widening, NH-48 Segment
Date of Inspection: 2026-09-02
Inspected By: Er. R. Menon, Engineer-in-Charge

Observations:

1. Structural steel for the superstructure has not yet arrived at site as of the inspection date.
The Contractor's site engineer confirmed the fabrication yard has completed only 60% of the
required members. This raises concern regarding the October 15, 2026 delivery obligation under
Section 7.2 of the Contract.

2. Concrete cube testing frequency for the completed pier caps was found to be below the
contractually required rate: only 4 sets were tested against an expected 7 sets for the volume
poured. Non-conformance noted; Contractor directed to increase testing frequency immediately.

3. Cement buffer stock at site was measured at approximately 9 days of consumption, below the
15-day minimum required under Section 7.5.

4. Safety Officer was present and weekly toolbox talk records were up to date. No safety
non-conformances noted.

5. Recommendation: Given current fabrication progress, there is a material risk that the
structural steel delivery milestone will be missed, which would trigger the penalty clause under
Section 7.2 and could cascade into delay of the overall Time for Completion (Section 9.4).
