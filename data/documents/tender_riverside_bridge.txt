NOTICE INVITING TENDER (NIT)
NIT No: SHDA/NH48/2025/RB-11
Work: Widening of Riverside Bridge, NH-48 Segment

1. The State Highways Development Authority invites e-bids from eligible contractors for the
above work. Estimated Contract Value: INR 42,50,00,000. Earnest Money Deposit: INR 85,00,000.

2. Bidders must have completed at least one bridge construction work of similar value in the
preceding seven years, and shall submit documentary evidence of annual financial turnover of not
less than INR 60,00,00,000 over the last three financial years.

3. The completion period for the Works shall be 18 months from the date of the Letter of Award.

4. Bid Security in the form specified above must remain valid for 180 days from the date of bid
opening. Bids received after the due date and time shall not be considered under any
circumstances.

5. The Authority reserves the right to accept or reject any or all bids without assigning any
reason thereof.

6. Technical bids shall be opened first; financial bids of technically qualified bidders shall be
opened on a subsequent date to be notified separately.
